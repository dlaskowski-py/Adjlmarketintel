How to read this

TDPS is a mean-reversion system from the 1970s: buy a 2-day oversold
reading inside a 50/200-day uptrend, stop at 3 ATR, scale 70% out on the
bounce and trail the rest. In our testing across 152 names and 27 years
it wins about 62% of trades. Its edge is concentrated in high-beta
technology; it does not work on large-cap dividend payers, where we
measure no reliable advantage at all. We size every position off the stop
distance so a loss costs the same regardless of the name's volatility.

These are signals from a quantitative system, not individual
recommendations, and they do not account for your tax situation,
time horizon, or existing holdings. Past results do not predict future
returns. Happy to walk through any of it.
